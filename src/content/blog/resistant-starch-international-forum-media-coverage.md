GreenWay Green Banana Flour / Resistant Starch is made from whole, peeled, green bananas. The raw bananas give the banana flour a slight green banana flavor, but no astringency and obvious banana flavor. you will enjoy the wholesome earthy flavor. GreenWay Green Banana Flour / Resistant Starch is paleo, gluten-free, grain-free, soy-free and vegan food ingredient and it is high in potassium which contains 880mg potassium per 100g. It can be used in everyday recipes, and directly substitute for wheat flour in certain cases. As a daily fibre supplement, GreenWay Green Banana Flour contains RS2 resistant starch that stimulates the growth and activity of beneficial microorganisms. Furthermore, it relieves constipation and increase the volume of defecation.
